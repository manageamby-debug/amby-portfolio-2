About me

I’m a Diploma in Computer Science student at Mbeya University of Science and Technology (MUST) in Tanzania. I started with Java and then learned Python systematically; today I build production-style full-stack apps and I’m learning Go.

I work in English and Kiswahili, so I design products that feel natural for Tanzanian users. I also work with my family’s insurance agency, Mwaluseke Insurance Agency.

Away from code, I compose and produce music in FL Studio. Sound design and arranging are a big part of how I think about building experiences.
